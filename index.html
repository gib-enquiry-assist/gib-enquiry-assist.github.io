Gib Enquiry Assist

Faster, accurate replies to property enquiries, for Gibraltar estate agents

I'm Will. I'm based in Gibraltar and I'm building a tool that helps local agencies keep up with the enquiries that come in through PropertyGibraltar and their own websites.

Where it's at: early testing. I'm talking to local agencies to learn how they handle enquiries today, and whether this would actually help.

What it does

  • Drafts a reply to each enquiry using only the listing details the agency has approved.
  • Suggests a viewing time, clearly marked as not confirmed until the agency confirms it.
  • Flags anything it can't answer (a price query, a property under offer, a request to speak to someone) for a person to handle.

How it fits in

  • It works alongside the tools agencies already use. It doesn't replace their website, CRM or portal listings.
  • A person always approves every reply. Nothing is sent automatically.
