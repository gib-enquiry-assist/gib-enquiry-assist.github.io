Gib Enquiry Assist

Faster, accurate replies for Gibraltar estate agents, across sales, lettings and property management

I'm Will. I'm based in Gibraltar and I'm building a tool that helps local agencies keep up with the messages that come in, from sales and lettings enquiries through PropertyGibraltar and their own websites to tenants of the properties they manage.

Where it's at: early testing. I'm talking to local agencies to learn how they handle enquiries today, and whether this would actually help.

What it does

Built to handle the whole enquiry, from first reply to booked viewing. Designed to send routine replies on its own, or you can choose to approve every message first. Anything it isn't sure about, like a price query or a complaint, always goes to a person.

Replies

  • Drafts a reply to each enquiry from PropertyGibraltar or your website, using only the listing details you've approved. WhatsApp enquiries are planned too.
  • Designed to draft replies out of hours and in Spanish, sent with or without your approval, your choice.
  • Designed to send the brochure, floor plan or EPC when asked, with or without your approval, your choice.
  • Flags anything it can't answer (a price query, a property under offer, a request to speak to someone) for a person to handle.

Viewings

  • Suggests viewing times. Being built to use your Google or Outlook calendar and add approved viewings to your diary.
  • Designed to send viewing confirmations and reminders, once a viewing is booked.

Keeping leads warm

  • Designed to suggest follow-ups for enquiries that have gone quiet.
  • Designed to suggest similar listings when a property isn't right or has gone under offer.
  • Planned: a weekly report on how many enquiries came in, how fast they were answered, and what needed a person.

Lettings and property management

  • Designed to log tenant maintenance requests and draft updates to tenants and contractors.
  • Designed to answer common tenant questions (bins, parking, who to call about a leak) from building information you've approved.
  • Designed to send rent and renewal reminders, with or without your approval, your choice.
  • Designed to flag urgent issues (a leak, no power, a safety concern) to a person straight away.

Gets smarter over time

  • Designed to offer to save your team's answer to a new question for next time. Building-wide facts (parking, pets, service charges) apply to every flat in that building. Saved answers are only reused once someone confirms them.
  • Designed to learn your team's tone and preferred wording from the edits they make to drafts.
  • Designed to spot questions that keep coming up, so you can add the answer once.
  • Designed to keep answers current when a listing changes (price drop, under offer, let agreed), so it doesn't repeat out-of-date details.

How it fits in

  • It works alongside the tools agencies already use. It doesn't replace their website, CRM or portal listings.
  • You choose how much it does on its own: let it send routine replies automatically, or approve every message first. In today's test version, every reply is drafted for approval.
